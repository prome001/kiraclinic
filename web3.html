About Kira Clinic

Kira Clinic was founded with one mission: to deliver accessible, compassionate, and high-quality healthcare to the community of Nyagatare District, Eastern Province, Rwanda. We believe that everyone deserves excellent medical care close to home.

Our modern facility is equipped with the latest diagnostic tools and staffed by a team of dedicated healthcare professionals who put patients first. Since opening our doors, we have served thousands of patients from Nyagatare and surrounding sectors including Gatunda, Karama, Karangazi, Katabagemu, Kiyombe, Matimba, and beyond.

  • Registered and licensed by the Rwanda Ministry of Health
  • Modern equipment and clean, comfortable environment
  • Affordable services with transparent pricing
  • Open 6 days a week with emergency on-call service
  • Community-focused health education programs
  • Partnerships with RBC and local hospitals
